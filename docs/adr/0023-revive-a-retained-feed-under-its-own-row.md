# Revive a retained Feed under its own row when the User subscribes to it again

Unsubscribing deletes the Subscription and leaves the Feed row for Retention to judge, which keeps it while Library saves still attribute to it. Subscribing again to a URL such a retained Feed holds, by hand or by OPML Import, revives that Feed row rather than creating a second Feed for the same URL (`subscriptions/new-subscription.ts` `recordSubscription`). Library items therefore keep the Feed identity they were saved from. The revived Subscription begins again: it is dated from the moment of subscribing, which the Feeds list's Recently added order and the Feed page's Subscribed date read, it is due immediately, and every preference, the Polling Interval, the Reading Source, a Custom Title and a Custom Description, starts from the default, because those were held by the Subscription that unsubscribing deleted. A new Feed, when nothing retained holds the URL, is named by the title OPML Import offers or by its URL's host until a retrieval reports its own title.

A retained Feed is found first through its URL aliases, then through its own entered and resolved URLs (`dormantFeedId`). The second lookup exists for a duplicate that a merge folded into another Feed (ADR 0007): the merge moved its aliases to the survivor, but its own URLs stay reserved to its row until Retention retires that row, so subscribing to them again still finds it. When it is revived that way, the URL is written back as its alias if no other Feed holds it.
