# Play the Digest's motion on the finished screen

The Digest's motion (`docs/DESIGN.md` §7) is played by `motion.ts` through Web Animations, never through state React renders. Every animation but a fold plays on DOM React has already committed in its final state: a new day's list and the days Show more adds animate after they render, and an unfold runs after its titles are in, given the list's height from before (`playUnfold`). An animation that is skipped, cancelled or never plays therefore leaves exactly the screen it would have ended on. That is also what lets `tests/client/setup.ts` replace `Element.prototype.animate` with one that has already finished, and `matchMedia` with a query that never matches, without hiding a bug. A fold is the exception, because the titles it removes must stay on screen while the list closes over them: `playFold` animates the height first, holding the end with `fill: 'forwards'`, then cuts the titles with `flushSync` and cancels the animation in the same turn, so the titles that stay never move.

Durations and curves are read from the stylesheet's tokens (`--duration-sheet-enter`, `--duration-sheet-exit`, `--ease-out`) with `getComputedStyle`, so CSS motion and script motion have one source of timing. `motion.ts` `duration` reads both `ms` and `s`, because the production CSS minifier rewrites `240ms` as `.24s`; reading only milliseconds played every Digest animation for a fraction of a millisecond in the production build and correctly in development (fixed in 9b45378).

An unfold or fold animates the list's height only as far as 32px past the bottom of the screen (`heightOnScreen`). A list that runs far past the screen would otherwise spend most of its easing out of sight, and the User would see only the fast middle of the curve; capping the travel shows the whole of it, and the rest of the height changes off screen. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the height changes at once and only the fades remain.

**Amended by ADR 0033.** `motion.ts` is unchanged and is played by `screens/digest-screen.tsx` (a new day and older days) and `screens/digest-layouts.tsx` `useFold` (a Firehose fold, as a row or a card). The duration and easing tokens it reads are set in `screens/screens.css`. There is no Summary box to fold.

**Amended (a fold holds still).** A fold animates its height on `--ease-drawer` (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`) rather than `--ease-out`, which finished most of a several-hundred-pixel travel in its first frames. The clip follows the fold's tone, its `::before` (`toneOf`): a `clip-path` cut at the tone's own edges, so the tone keeps its bleed while it moves and the rows never show past it. It is the clip-path alone: `overflow: clip` needs `overflow-clip-margin` to spare the bleed, and Safari ignores that margin, so the tone lost its sides for the length of the move. Without `overflow` the hidden rows still lie in the page, so the fold sets `overflow-anchor: none` while it moves and scroll anchoring never holds on to a row the clip hides. Folding lands on an element both states share, passed as `kept` from `useFold`: a row fold's head, a fold card's first title. Both are drawn alike folded and unfolded (the row's latest title is cut to one line; the card lists its latest title as the first of its titles), and the drawer's spacing lies below them, so `playFold` ends on the folded height exactly and the cut changes nothing on screen. The titles an unfold fades in are the ones after `kept`.

Two layout rules exist for this motion. Columns deals its cards into stacks (`Stacks` in `screens/digest-layouts.tsx`) rather than CSS multi-column, because multi-column rebalances on every frame of a height animation and moved the unfolding card into another column mid-flight. And on the Wall a fold card hangs from the top of its row, because a card held to the bottom line grows upward.
